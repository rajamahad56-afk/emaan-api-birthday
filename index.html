Dear Appa, 🥹💗

Happy Birthday to one of the most special people in my life. I might not say it every day, and maybe I don't always know how to explain it, but you mean so much to me. Having you as my sister is a blessing I will always be thankful for.

Appa, life gets busy and sometimes we forget to say the things that matter most. So today, I just want you to know that you are loved more than words can explain. Tum meri life ka bohat special hissa ho, and no matter how much we grow up, you will always have a place in my heart that nobody else can take.

Thank you for being you — for the little moments, the memories, the laughter, and even the silly things that somehow become our favourite stories. Har waqt perfect hona zaroori nahi hota; bas tumhara meri life mein hona hi mere liye bohat meaningful hai.

I pray that Allah gives you endless happiness, good health, peace, and success. May every beautiful dua in your heart come true, may your smile always stay bright, and may life bring you the same kindness and love that you deserve. Allah tumhein hamesha khush rakhay, buri nazar se bachaye, aur tumhari zindagi ko khoobsurat cheezon se bhar de. Ameen. 🤍

And Appa, whenever life feels heavy, remember that you never have to be strong every single second. You deserve care, comfort, and all the happiness in the world too.

Happy Birthday, Emaaan Apii. I may not always say it perfectly, but I love you so much. You will always be my Appa, and I'll always be cheering for you. Here's to more memories, more laughter, and a year full of beautiful surprises. 🎂🎀
